## Design implications

Based on this persona, translate their profile into concrete website requirements:

| Persona trait | Design implication |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Introvert / risk-averse | Provide self-service information; minimize pressure to call |
| Analytical | Use statistics, evidence, case study data |
| Busy | Keep page short; lead with value; scannable sections |
| Values social proof | Show customer quotes and logos early in the page |
| Frustrated by jargon | Use plain language in hero and feature descriptions |
