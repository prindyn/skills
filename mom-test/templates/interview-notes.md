## Raw Notes

> Capture what they actually said — not your interpretation. Use their words when possible. If they said something interesting, write the quote verbatim even if it's messy.

Use these tags inline to flag signal type as you write:

- `[FACT]` — past behavior, specific event, resource already spent
- `[FLUFF]` — generic opinion, future-tense, hypothetical, compliment
- `[COMMIT]` — time, money, or reputation committed
- `[QUOTE]` — verbatim quote worth keeping
- `[FOLLOW-UP]` — something worth digging into in the next call or follow-up

```
[Write notes here during or immediately after the call]

Examples:
- [FACT] Missed a SaaS renewal in January, triggered a $12k auto-charge. Didn't realize until saw it on the credit card statement three weeks later.
- [FLUFF] "I think this kind of thing happens to a lot of companies."
- [QUOTE] "It's not that tracking is hard — it's that nobody owns it. Everyone assumes someone else is watching."
- [FACT] Currently uses a Google Sheet shared with 3 people. Last updated 2 months ago. No reminders set.
- [COMMIT - TIME] Offered to let me observe her next vendor review meeting. "It's in two weeks, I'll send a calendar invite."
- [FLUFF] "I'd definitely use something automated for this."
- [FACT] Spent ~4 hours last quarter reconciling which contracts were active after a team member left.
- [FOLLOW-UP] She mentioned their VP of Finance is "really stressed about vendor sprawl" — potential second interview.
```
