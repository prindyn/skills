## 5. What Good Data Would Look Like

> Before you talk to anyone, describe what would make you confident the problem is real and worth solving. This prevents you from moving goalposts after the fact.

**Evidence that would make me confident:**
```
[e.g., "3+ people independently describe missing a renewal deadline or paying for something they didn't want; at least one has already built a workaround; at least one has existing budget or has paid for something adjacent"]
```

**Evidence that would make me pause or pivot:**
```
[e.g., "Nobody has experienced this personally; the problem only comes up when I ask directly; nobody has tried to solve it"]
```
